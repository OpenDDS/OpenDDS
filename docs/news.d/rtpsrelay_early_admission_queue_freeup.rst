.. news-start-section: Additions
.. news-start-section: RtpsRelay
- A new option ``-EarlyAdmissionQueueFreeup`` is added to specify whether the relay can remove an entry from the admission queue
  early, before the admission queue duration has expired, when the relay detects approximately that the corresponding client
  participant may be disconnecting from the relay.
